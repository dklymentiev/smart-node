# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in Smart Node to be safe, respectful, and productive for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

Examples of welcome behavior include constructive feedback, clear technical
disagreement, respect for boundaries, and taking responsibility for mistakes.
Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, issue and review threads,
project communication channels, and public spaces where someone is representing
the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `security@klymentiev.com`. Include links or
screenshots when safe to do so. Reports will be reviewed confidentially; the
maintainer may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude a participant
depending on severity and pattern. Retaliation against a reporter is itself a
violation.

If the report concerns the person receiving that mailbox, use GitHub's abuse
reporting tools instead.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
